About the journal

Homes are personal. Good advice should be, too.

Habitat Journal is an independent digital publication for people who want their homes to feel calmer, work harder and reflect a life actually lived.

Our point of view

We look beyond the showroom.

We cover interiors, household technology, kitchens, workspaces and the practical decisions that shape daily life. Our work begins with a simple question: will this idea remain useful after the novelty wears off?

That means considering real rooms, real constraints and real routines. We favour clarity over hype, materials that age well, and technology that quietly removes friction instead of creating more of it.

Editorial scope

What we cover.

Smart home: connected products that make routines simpler, safer or more efficient.

Design: approachable principles for proportion, colour, light and material.

Kitchen: tools, appliances and layouts that support the way people really cook.

Workspace: thoughtful setups for focus, comfort and better boundaries.

How we research and review

Who publishes the journal

A small, independent editorial desk.

Habitat Journal is a founder-led digital publication. It is currently researched, written and maintained independently rather than by a large newsroom or retail company.

Stories carrying the “Habitat Journal editors” byline are produced by the publication’s editorial desk. Product research articles state whether the work is based on direct use, manufacturer documentation or comparative desk research. We do not claim hands-on testing where none took place.

Questions about ownership, editorial work or commercial partnerships can be sent directly to editor@habitatjournal.com.

How readers find us

Editorial first, distributed with care.

Habitatjournal.com is our primary publishing and promotional channel. We create original English-language features, buying guidance and product research for readers making decisions about their homes.

Our distribution is built around direct readership, search discovery and carefully selected promotion. Where a partner permits paid search or other performance marketing, we follow the individual programme terms, including trademark, bidding and landing-page restrictions.

We do not use misleading redirects, fabricated discounts, unsolicited bulk email or claims of first-hand testing that did not occur. Our aim is to send readers to a merchant only after giving them enough context to make an informed choice.
