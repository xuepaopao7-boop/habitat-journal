Our standards

Our standards

Judgement before promotion.

The principles that guide what we publish, how we assess products and how we correct the record.

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Editorial purpose

Habitat Journal helps readers make more considered decisions about the spaces, objects and technology around them. We publish reported features, practical guides, reviews and opinion grounded in clear reasoning.

Research and evaluation

We begin with the reader’s use case, not a product’s marketing claims. Our assessments may draw on direct use, specification analysis, manufacturer documentation, expert consultation, comparative research and credible owner feedback. Articles should make the basis of an assessment clear.

How we label our work

Product research means a desk-based assessment using current product documentation, support material and comparative context. It is not presented as hands-on testing. Buying guides explain a decision framework and may use public standards or expert guidance. Reviews are reserved for work that includes direct use or clearly identified first-hand evaluation.

Whenever a story relies substantially on manufacturer claims, we link to the relevant source and describe the limitations of our assessment. Images used for editorial presentation do not, by themselves, imply that a product was tested in the pictured room.

Visual sourcing and rights

Editorial images may be created for Habitat Journal, generated as original editorial artwork, licensed from a photography source, or supplied by a manufacturer or rights holder for editorial use. We use visual material only when we have a reasonable basis to publish it and keep source or licence information with our editorial records.

Generated or illustrative images are used to communicate a room, product category or design idea; they are not evidence of a hands-on product test. Manufacturer images are identified by context where that distinction matters. Rights holders may contact us through the contact page with a specific image address and supporting information.

Recommendations

A recommendation must explain who the choice suits, the problem it solves and the trade-offs involved. We do not describe a product as universally “best” when fit depends on room size, budget, household needs or personal preference.

Commercial relationships

Advertising, sponsorship and affiliate relationships are kept separate from editorial conclusions. Commercial partners may not approve, rewrite or suppress an independent assessment. Material relationships are disclosed to readers.

Traffic acquisition must also follow the terms of the individual merchant or affiliate programme. We do not bid on a partner's trademarks, present ourselves as the merchant, or use restricted promotion methods unless the partner has given explicit permission.

Accuracy and corrections

We aim to verify names, prices, product details and material claims before publication. Information can change. When we identify a meaningful error, we correct it promptly and, where useful, note what changed.

Research-led product stories are checked against primary sources wherever practical. Where specifications, subscription terms or compatibility can change, readers are directed to the manufacturer’s current documentation before purchase.

Updates

Buying guidance should remain useful after publication. We may update articles when products are discontinued, important specifications change or stronger alternatives become available. The publication or update date appears on each story.

Reader feedback

Readers, brands and subject-matter experts may raise an accuracy concern through our contact page. Evidence-based feedback is reviewed by the editorial team.
