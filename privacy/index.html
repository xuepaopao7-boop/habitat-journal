Policy

Privacy policy.

What this website collects, what it does not, and how external services may handle data.

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Overview

Habitat Journal is a static editorial website. At present, we do not offer user accounts, accept payments, or operate a database that stores visitor profiles.

Information you choose to send

The contact form on this website opens a draft in your own email application. Information entered into that form is not transmitted to or stored by Habitat Journal’s website. If you send the email, your email provider and the receiving email service will process the message under their own privacy terms.

Technical information

Our hosting and security provider may process basic technical data, such as IP addresses, browser information and security events, to deliver the site, prevent abuse and maintain reliability. This processing is performed as part of standard web hosting and security operations.

Cookies and analytics

Habitat Journal does not currently set first-party advertising cookies or operate personalised advertising. If analytics or other measurement tools are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the service, purpose and available controls.

External and affiliate links

Articles may link to third-party websites. Those services may collect data or use cookies under their own policies. Habitat Journal does not control how an external website processes information after you leave our site.

Children

This publication is intended for a general adult audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the publication develops. The date above will reflect the latest material update.

Questions

Privacy-related questions can be sent through our contact page.
